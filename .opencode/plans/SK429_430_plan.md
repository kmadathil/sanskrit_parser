# SK429/430 Implementation Plan

## Understanding

### SK429 (3.2.60): त्यदादिषु दृशोऽनालोचने कञ्च

**Natural Siddha** The structure `[sarvanAma_pada | in_context(dfS) | kvin]` inherently produces the तादृक्ष-type forms. yaṇ transforms तद्→तादृ (य|दृ→य), and SK430 provides the final ए→आ antyadeśa. No separate YAML rule needed.

### SK430 (6.3.91): आ सर्वनाम्नः

**Antyādeśa:** आ replaces the ए of a sarvanAma stem before kvin.

The Ashtadhyayi says आ comes after a sarvanAma. At the juncture where तादृ (from तद् after yaṇ) meets दृश् (with `?samAsa`), तादृ carries `?sarvanAma_pada` and दृश् carries `?samAsa`. SK430 fires here: ए→आ (l→आ), giving तादृशा.

**Derivation trace for `[tad_pada, in_context(dfS, "samAsa"), in_context(kvin, "samAsa"), su]`:**

| Step | Window | yaṇ | SK430 | Join |
|------|--------|-----|-------|------|
| 1 | (तद्\|दृश्) | yaṇ: तद्→तादृ | — | तादृदृश, तादृ has ?sarvanAma_pada |
| 2 | (तादृ\|दृश्) | — | SK430: तादृ→तादृशा (lp:?sarvanAma_pada, rp:?samAsa → ए→आ) | तादृशा |
| 3 | (तादृशा\|kvin) | — | SK430 fires if kvin has ?samAsa → तादृशा | kvin joins (empty): तादृशा |
| 4 | (तादृशा\|su) | yaṇ at य\|अ→यादृशाय | — | तादृशाय |

Then pada-end changes on तादृशाय:
- ट stays ट
- ष at pada-end: ṣatva (श→ष) → ष stays ष; ku (ष→क) → तादृक्षाय
- Sandhi: य\|आ→या, ण\|य→ण → तादृक्षाय → तादृक्षाय
